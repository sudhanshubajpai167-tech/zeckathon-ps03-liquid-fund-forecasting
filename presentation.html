Pinball Loss (Quantile Loss)

Lα(y, q̂) = max(α·(y−q̂), (1−α)·(q̂−y)) — asymmetric scoring for each quantile forecast level (q0.025 → q0.975)

Deliverable 04 · Decision Layer

Cash Buffer Optimisation — Cost-Driven Policy

Total Cost = Yield Drag on Idle Cash  +  2× Shortfall Penalty on Redemptions

❌ Naïve Baseline

Hold last year's same-month outflow. Simple but ignores current macro conditions. Higher shortfall risk.

~45% shortfall freq.

📊 P95 Outflow Policy

Hold cash = 95th percentile forecast outflow, floored at 3% of AUM. Model-driven, conservative.

~5% shortfall freq.

✅ Adaptive Policy

Tax months → P95 × 1.10 with 5% floor. Non-tax months → P95 with 2% floor. Context-aware and optimal.
